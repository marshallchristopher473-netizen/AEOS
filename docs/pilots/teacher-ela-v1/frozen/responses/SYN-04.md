# SYN-04

Synthetic response written for the AEOS teacher ELA pilot v1.0. It is not student work.

Marrowfield's plan was a good idea because it gave bees food all season while also respecting residents' concerns. The students did not plant just any flowers. They chose native wildflowers that bloom at different times, so bees could find food from spring until fall. This matters because a strip that bloomed for only a few weeks would leave bees hungry for the rest of the year.

The council also listened to people who were worried. When some residents feared more stings, the council kept the strips away from playgrounds and put up signs explaining the project. Because of this, the plan could keep going instead of being stopped by complaints. It worked, too: the bee count rose to 480, higher than the first count of 410, which shows that a steady food supply brought the bees back.
