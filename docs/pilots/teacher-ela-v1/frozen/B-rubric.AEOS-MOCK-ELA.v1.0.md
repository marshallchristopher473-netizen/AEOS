# AEOS Mock ELA Rubric v1.0

Analytic rubric for Assignment A v1.0 only. Four dimensions, each scored 0–3. Total = sum of the four dimension scores (0–12). The total is derived. It is never scored on its own.

This rubric was written for a synthetic usability pilot. It has not been validated, aligned to any state assessment, or tested for reliability. Do not use it to grade real student work.

## Definitions used by every dimension

- **Detail:** a fact, number, place, or action that the response attributes to the passage.
- **Accurate:** the passage states it. A detail that contradicts the passage, or that the passage does not contain, is **inaccurate**.
- **Specific:** the detail names the particular fact, number, place, or action. Example: "gardeners reported larger harvests of squash and berries."
- **General:** the detail refers to something the passage says without naming the particular fact, number, place, or action. Example: "it helped the gardens."
- **Error (Conventions):** one misspelled word, one wrong word form (for example *then* for *than*, *its* for *it's*), one missing capital at the start of a sentence or on "I", one subject–verb agreement error, or one missing end mark or run-on join. Count each instance once.

## Claim

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 3 | States a clear position on whether the plan was a good idea **and** names the reason(s) for that position in the claim. |
| 2 | States a clear position but does not name a reason in the claim. |
| 1 | The position is unclear, contradictory, or only implied. |
| 0 | No position. |

## Evidence

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 3 | Two or more accurate, relevant, **specific** details. |
| 2 | Exactly one accurate, relevant, specific detail. Any other details are accurate but general. |
| 1 | No accurate specific detail, but at least one accurate general detail. |
| 0 | No accurate detail from the passage. |

**Cap:** if the response presents any inaccurate detail, Evidence is capped at 1.

## Reasoning

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 3 | Explains how **each** cited detail supports the claim. |
| 2 | Explains how at least one, but not every, cited detail supports the claim. |
| 1 | Lists or restates details, or offers general opinion, without explaining how any cited detail supports the claim. |
| 0 | No details and no reasoning. |

## Conventions

| Score | Descriptor |
|---:|---|
| 3 | 0–1 errors, and none affects meaning. |
| 2 | 2–5 errors, and the meaning of every sentence is clear. |
| 1 | 6 or more errors, **or** any error that makes a sentence's meaning unclear. |
| 0 | Errors make most of the response unreadable. |

## Scoring rules

1. Score the Claim on the position statement. A reason that first appears later in the response does not raise the Claim score.
2. Score the Evidence and Reasoning dimensions on content only. Conventions errors do not lower them unless the meaning cannot be recovered.
3. A sentence whose meaning cannot be recovered contributes no detail to Evidence.
4. Record each credited detail with the passage paragraph(s) it comes from (¶1–¶5).
