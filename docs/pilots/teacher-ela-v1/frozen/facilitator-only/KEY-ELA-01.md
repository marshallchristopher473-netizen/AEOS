Revise the response or the key, re-version, re-hash, and re-approve (packet §7).

## How to read the evidence tables

- **Status** says whether an item is accurate or inaccurate and whether a teacher's final record must include it.
  - `required`: the final evidence record must list this item with one of the accepted locations.
  - `optional`: the record may list it or leave it out. Either is agreement.
  - `must be marked not credited`: the record must show this detail as inaccurate and not credited.
- **Accepted locations**: the passage paragraph sets that count as correct. Alternatives are separated by "or".
- Quotations are copied verbatim from the response file.
