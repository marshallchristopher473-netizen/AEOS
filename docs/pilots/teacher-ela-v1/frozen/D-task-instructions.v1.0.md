# Teacher task instructions and final decision sheet (v1.0)

The facilitator reads the instructions for the assigned task aloud, word for word, then hands over the materials. The only clarification allowed is re-reading these instructions. The facilitator gives no coaching on content or the rubric.

## Materials for every task

- Assignment A v1.0 (passage ¶1–¶5 and the task)
- AEOS Mock ELA Rubric v1.0
- The three synthetic responses in your assigned set
- One final decision sheet per response (below)

## Task A: assisted workflow

> For each of your three responses you will also get a workflow output card. The card suggests a score for each rubric dimension and lists evidence it found, with passage locations. The cards were written ahead of time for this activity. They are not produced by AI, and they may contain mistakes. Checking them is part of the task.
>
> For each response: read the response, review the card, check each suggested score against the rubric, and check each cited piece of evidence against the response and the passage. Then fill in the final decision sheet. You may accept a row as written, change it, add something it left out, or remove something. Your final decision sheet is the record, not the card. Tell me when your final entry for the third response is complete.

## Task B: manual workflow

> For each of your three responses, read the response and score it with the rubric. There is no workflow output in this task. Fill in the final decision sheet for each response. Tell me when your final entry for the third response is complete.

## What counts as a correction (Task A, facilitator use)

Compare the final decision sheet with the response's workflow output card. Count **one correction** for each of these:

1. A dimension score that differs from the card's suggested score.
2. A dimension score added that the card left out.
3. An evidence row removed, or added, compared with the card.
4. An evidence row whose passage location differs from the card's.
5. An evidence row whose credited status differs from the card's.

The total does not count separately, because it follows from the dimension scores. The card's "specific/general" labels and rationale text are not part of the final record, so changing or ignoring them is not a correction.
