**Bound:** the deliverable may promise only what the inspected artifact contains, after the gaps are fixed or
disclosed. Anything else is out of scope.

## 3. Exclusions (not part of this offer)

- Live support, coaching, or classroom observation.
- AI-generated content, AI grading, or access to any AEOS software or account.
- Customization beyond the fields in section 2.
- Any claim about student learning, time saved, standards alignment beyond what the artifact states, or
  implementation results. None of these have evidence.
- District, school, or purchase-order sales. This test is for individual teachers paying personally.

## 4. Onboarding (buyer steps)

1. Buyer reads this page and the terms, then pays through the verified checkout.
2. Seller sends the files to the buyer's email within the stated window: ______.
3. Buyer confirms receipt. Seller logs delivery in the offer-test log.
4. Seller sends one optional, anonymous feedback request after ______ days. No reply is required.

## 5. Data-handling limits

- The buyer is told in writing: **do not send student names, work, scores, or records.** If any arrive, the seller
  deletes them, does not open attachments further, and records only "student data received and deleted" in the log.
- The seller keeps only buyer name, email, payment reference and delivery date, in the private store.
- No buyer or prospect data is committed to GitHub.

## 6. Proceed / stop conditions

**PROCEED to the offer test only when every box is checked:**

- [ ] Every section 1 and section 2 field is filled and dated.
- [ ] Each documented artifact gap is FIXED or DISCLOSED.
- [ ] Test-mode checkout and test-mode refund both passed ([protocol §3](02_OFFER_TEST_PROTOCOL.md#3-test-mode-checkout-and-refund-gate)).
- [ ] Decision thresholds are pre-registered ([protocol §2](02_OFFER_TEST_PROTOCOL.md#2-pre-registration-fill-before-the-first-contact)).
- [ ] DO has confirmed that selling this privately does not conflict with any current employer's outside-activity rules.

**STOP immediately if any of these happen**, and record it in the log:

- A buyer needs student data to use the deliverable.
- Making the sale would require a claim that section 3 excludes.
- A refund cannot be issued within the stated policy.
- A buyer asks for a district, school, or purchase-order purchase.
- Delivery fails twice for the same buyer.
