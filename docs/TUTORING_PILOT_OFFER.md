# Free Inquiry Audit — One-Page Pilot Offer

**For:** owners and directors of tutoring businesses
**The ask:** 20 old inquiries. Nothing else.

---

## The problem

Every tutoring business loses families it never knew it lost.

An inquiry arrives at 9pm on a Sunday. Nobody sees it until Tuesday. By then the
parent has booked somewhere else. Another arrives with no grade and no subject,
so answering it takes three back-and-forth messages nobody has time for. A
parent whose child has a test on Friday gets the same reply, in the same order,
as a parent who is casually comparing prices.

None of this shows up in a report. A lost inquiry leaves no trace — which is
exactly why it keeps happening.

## What we do

We read every inquiry the moment it arrives and tell you:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it is** | A new family, a current client, a price shopper, a job applicant, or spam |
| **Whether you can serve them** | Qualified, or still missing something |
| **How fast to move** | Urgent, high, medium or low — an exam on Friday outranks a rate question |
| **What you still need to ask** | Grade, subject, contact, availability, start date, goal |
| **What to do next** | Book a consultation, send rates, ask two questions, escalate, or archive |
| **A reply you can send** | Drafted for your review. Nothing is sent without you |

## The offer: a free shadow-mode audit

**Send us 20 old inquiries. We will show you what our system would have done
with each one, at no cost and at no risk to a single customer relationship.**

Shadow mode means:

- ❌ We do **not** contact your families.
- ❌ We do **not** connect to your CRM, calendar, phone or email.
- ❌ You do **not** change how you work.
- ✅ You send historical inquiries with names and contact details removed.
- ✅ We run them and send you a comparison: **what your team actually did** vs
  **what the system recommends**.

You keep the report whether or not you ever work with us. If it shows nothing
useful, you have lost an hour and learned that your intake is in good shape —
which is worth knowing.

## What you get back

A short written report:

1. **Inquiry-by-inquiry comparison** — your action vs the recommended action.
2. **Agreement rate** — how often we match a decision your team already made
   well. This is the honesty check: if we disagree with you constantly, we are
   probably wrong.
3. **Missed-urgency list** — inquiries we flag urgent or qualified that waited,
   or were never answered. Usually the most uncomfortable page, and the one
   worth the hour.
4. **Missing-information patterns** — what parents consistently leave out, which
   is usually a fixable problem with your intake form rather than a software
   purchase.
5. **An honest verdict** on whether this would help your business, including if
   the answer is no.

## Data handling

This matters more than the software, so it is in writing:

- **Anonymize before sending.** Remove student and parent names, phone numbers,
  email addresses and street addresses. We only need the message text, the
  channel, and the date received. We will send a template and help strip the
  file if that is easier.
- **No student records.** Do not send grades, assessment results, IEP or 504
  documents, or anything from a student information system. We do not need them
  and will not accept them.
- **Storage and deletion.** Inquiries are held only for the audit, in a private
  workspace, and deleted within 30 days of the walkthrough. Say the word earlier
  and they go immediately.
- **No third parties, no training.** Your inquiries are not shared with anyone
  and are not used to train any model.
- **Written confirmation** of all of the above before you send a single file.

If your business serves students under 13 or handles school-contracted work,
tell us early — COPPA and FERPA considerations change what is appropriate to
send, and we would rather raise that ourselves than have you discover it later.

## What we can prove today, and what we cannot

Straight answers, because you will ask and because the honest version is the
more persuasive one:

**What we have tested.** The system has been scored against 100 standardized
parent inquiries — urgent requests, vague one-liners, price shoppers, spam,
special-education cases, current clients, and deliberately awkward edge cases.
It gets **98% of them right across all five decisions**, with **zero cases**
where an urgent family was deprioritized or a spam message was escalated to
staff. Every decision is traceable to the specific rule that produced it, so
when it is wrong, we can show you exactly why and fix it.

**What we have not tested.** Those 100 inquiries were written by us. No real
tutoring business's mail has gone through this yet. We cannot tell you it will
increase your enrollment, save you a specific number of hours, or match the way
your particular families write — because nobody has measured that.

**Your 20 inquiries are how we find out.** You are not buying a proven result.
You are getting a free look at your own intake, and we are getting the first
real test of whether this holds up. If it does not, you will be the first to
know, from us.
