# Coverage Pay Inquiry — Draft

An open loop worth closing. It may recover pay for work already completed, and unlike
everything else in this module it requires no buyer and no sample.

**Important:** whether a pay correction is actually owed has not been verified here. The
draft below asks a question and requests records. It does not assert that you were
underpaid. Make that claim only after you have seen the records and confirmed the
discrepancy yourself.

## Before sending

- [ ] List the specific dates you provided coverage.
- [ ] Note the class or assignment covered for each date, if you have it.
- [ ] Check your district's coverage-pay provision — usually in the negotiated agreement
      or an employee handbook. Know the rate and the conditions before you ask.
- [ ] Check whether a claim deadline applies. Many agreements have one.
- [ ] Decide who to send it to: usually your building administrator or the payroll
      office. If unsure, ask your building secretary who handles coverage-pay questions —
      a one-line question, not an escalation.
- [ ] Keep the tone neutral. This is a records question, not a dispute. Disputes are
      slower and cost more goodwill than the amount at stake usually justifies.

## Draft

**Subject:** Question about coverage pay for [MONTH] / [DATE RANGE]

Hello [NAME],

I am reviewing my records and wanted to check on coverage pay for a few dates this
[semester/year] when I covered classes outside my assigned schedule.

The dates I have are:

- [DATE] — [CLASS / PERIOD / TEACHER COVERED]
- [DATE] — [CLASS / PERIOD / TEACHER COVERED]
- [DATE] — [CLASS / PERIOD / TEACHER COVERED]

Could you let me know whether these were submitted for coverage pay, and if so, which
pay period they appeared in? If anything is missing on my end — a form I should have
filed or a different process I should be using — I would appreciate knowing that as well,
so I can handle it correctly going forward.

Thank you,

Christopher Marshall
[CONTACT INFORMATION]

## Notes on the draft

- It asks whether the dates were submitted. It does not claim they were not paid. If
  they were paid and you missed it, you have not accused anyone of anything.
- It offers the possibility that you missed a step. This makes it easy for the other
  person to answer honestly without defensiveness, which is usually the fastest route
  to the money.
- It requests specific information — which pay period — so the reply is actionable
  rather than "I'll look into it."
- Send it once. If there is no reply in five business days, send one short follow-up.
  If there is still no reply, ask your building administrator in person who should
  receive it.

## Definition of done

Either a written answer about each date, or a documented second attempt and a decision
to let it go. Log the time spent in
[`../evidence/time-and-income-log.csv`](../evidence/time-and-income-log.csv) under the
`coverage_pay` experiment, and log any recovered amount as income. If it recovers
several hundred dollars for one hour of work, that is the best effective rate in this
entire module — and worth knowing.
