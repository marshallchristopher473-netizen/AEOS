# Deck Sprint — Intake Form

Send this once the deposit clears. **The seven-business-day clock starts when this comes
back complete**, not when it is sent. An incomplete intake is the second-largest hour
sink after unbounded research; do not start building to fill the gaps yourself.

---

## Section 1 — The event

1. What is the event, and what is the date?
2. How long is your slot, in minutes?
3. Where is it being delivered — a room with a projector, a video call, or both?
4. Who is presenting? You, someone else on your team, or several people?

## Section 2 — The audience

5. Who is in the room? Be specific: "parents of 6th–8th graders, mostly first-time
   attendees" tells me more than "parents."
6. What does this audience already know about the topic?
7. What do you want them to **do** after the presentation? One sentence, one action.
8. What would make this presentation a failure in your eyes?

## Section 3 — The material

9. Attach your approved source material (up to ten pages).
10. Is there anything in it you must keep word-for-word — a required statement, a
    compliance line, a quoted policy?
11. Is there anything in it you would rather cut if space is tight?
12. Do you have a slide template, brand colors, or a logo I should use? If not, I will
    use a clean default and tell you what I chose.

## Section 4 — Confirmations

Please confirm each:

- [ ] I have the right to use and share all submitted material.
- [ ] The material contains **no student records, IEP content, identifiable student work,
      or other confidential educational information.**
- [ ] Factual claims in my material are mine to stand behind.
- [ ] I understand the project includes 8–10 content slides, one visual style, speaker
      notes, and one consolidated revision round.
- [ ] I understand that additional slides, new research, a change of audience, or a
      second revision round are quoted separately.

## Section 5 — Logistics

13. Who is my single point of contact for feedback? (One person. Committee feedback
    arriving from three directions is how a fixed-price project loses money.)
14. What is the latest date you can return a consolidated revision list?

---

## Internal checklist — before you start building

Do not open the slide file until all of these are true:

- [ ] Deposit cleared.
- [ ] All fourteen questions answered.
- [ ] Source material received and under ten pages.
- [ ] All five confirmations checked.
- [ ] Material contains no confidential educational information — **you have verified this
      yourself, not just taken the checkbox.**
- [ ] Audience and desired action are specific enough to make structural decisions from.
- [ ] Single point of contact named.
- [ ] Delivery date calculated and written down: seven business days from today.
- [ ] Start time logged in `evidence/time-and-income-log.csv`.

If any box is unchecked, send one message naming exactly what is missing. Do not start
and backfill. Starting early with a half-intake is what turns a 10-hour project into a
25-hour one.
