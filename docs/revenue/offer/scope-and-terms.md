# Deck Sprint — Scope and Terms (internal)

This is the document that protects the hourly rate. The offer one-pager is what the
client reads; this is what you hold yourself to.

A fixed price only works when the scope is fixed. $650 over 10 hours is $65/hour.
The same $650 over 25 hours is $26/hour — worse than the fallback options and not worth
the evening. Every rule below exists to keep the hour count near 10.

## The target budget

| Phase | Target hours |
| --- | --- |
| Sales conversation and scope confirmation | 1.5 |
| Intake review and outline | 1.5 |
| Slide build | 4.0 |
| Speaker notes | 1.5 |
| One revision round | 1.0 |
| Admin, invoicing, handoff | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **10.0** |

Log against these. If any phase runs 50% over, that is the signal to re-read this file
before continuing, not after.

## Included

- 8–10 content slides, plus a title slide (the title slide does not count toward ten)
- One visual style: one font pairing, one color set, one layout system
- Speaker notes on every content slide
- One consolidated revision round
- Final delivery as `.pptx` or a Google Slides copy owned by the client

## Explicitly excluded

Each of these is a separate quote. Say the number before the work, never after.

| Excluded | Why it is excluded |
| --- | --- |
| Original research or fact-finding | Unbounded; the single largest hour sink |
| Writing new content from scratch | The offer converts existing material; it does not author it |
| Slides beyond ten | Each additional slide carries build plus notes plus revision |
| Changing the audience after intake | Audience drives every structural decision; a change is a rebuild |
| A second revision round | One consolidated round is the whole basis of the price |
| Source verification of client claims | You confirm your own sources; see below |
| Custom illustration, animation, or video | Outside the offer |
| Live delivery, facilitation, or rehearsal | Outside the offer |
| Rush turnaround under seven business days | Separate quote, priced as a surcharge |
| Branding, logo, or template design | Outside the offer |

## Source material rules

- Up to ten pages, counted as submitted. Eleven pages is a conversation, not a silent yes.
- The client must have the right to use and share the material.
- **No student records, IEP content, identifiable student work, or other confidential
  educational information.** If it arrives, stop, do not open it further than needed to
  identify it, and tell the client you cannot accept it. Re-scope around non-confidential
  material or decline the project.
- Claims of fact in client material stay the client's responsibility. You present them;
  you do not warrant them. If a claim looks wrong, flag it once in writing and let the
  client decide.

## Revisions

One consolidated round means one list, submitted once.

- The client gathers all feedback into a single message before you touch the file.
- Feedback arriving in pieces is held until the list is complete; the clock does not run
  while you wait.
- A revision adjusts wording, order, emphasis, and styling within the existing structure.
- A revision that changes the audience, the slide count, or the argument is a new scope.

Script when feedback exceeds the round:

> Happy to make these. Items 1–6 fit the revision round and are included. Items 7 and 8
> change the structure, so they fall outside it — I can do both for $X and it adds two
> business days. Want me to proceed with 1–6 now and you decide on the rest?

## Payment

- **50% deposit ($325)** before work starts. The deposit reserves the delivery week and
  is non-refundable once intake is accepted.
- **50% balance ($325)** on delivery of the final file, due within seven days.
- Work does not start on a verbal yes. It starts when the deposit clears.
- The editable file transfers on final payment. The review copy may be shared before then.

## Change orders

Anything outside this document follows the same three steps, in writing, every time:

1. Name what changed.
2. Name the price and the added days.
3. Get a written yes before doing the work.

An unwritten change order is unpaid work.

## Cancellation

If the client cancels after intake is accepted, the deposit is retained. If you cancel,
the deposit is refunded in full.

## Decision rule

If a prospect negotiates the price below $500, or asks for an exclusion to be included
for free, decline politely. A discounted first project sets the rate for every project
after it, and this experiment exists to measure whether $650 is achievable — not whether
something cheaper is.
